Privacy Notice

Squeebs is a private, non-commercial personal-finance application operated by its owner for personal use. This notice describes how financial information is handled in that personal workflow.

Information handled

Squeebs may process account and transaction information, including transaction dates, descriptions, amounts, currencies, account balances, and the owner's categorisations and financial plans. Information may come from statements the owner imports or from connections the owner explicitly authorises.

Bank connections and service providers

When the owner chooses to connect an account through Enable Banking, the bank and Enable Banking handle the authorisation and account-information retrieval process according to their own applicable terms and privacy notices. Squeebs uses the resulting account information for personal tracking, reporting and cashflow planning. The owner can choose whether and when to establish such a connection.

Squeebs does not initiate payments or transfers, and the owner enters banking authentication information only within the bank's or provider's authorisation flow, not on this public website.

Storage and sharing

Financial records and classification data are maintained in the owner's private, local/self-hosted Squeebs environment, including any private local backups. They are not published through this site, sold, or used for advertising. Relevant data passes through the bank and authorised integration providers when the owner requests a connection or retrieval.

Retention and access revocation

The owner controls the local records and their retention. A bank connection may be revoked through the bank or relevant provider. Revoking consent stops future authorised retrieval, but does not automatically remove financial records already stored locally; these are managed separately by the owner.

Security

Credentials, access tokens, application signing keys and private financial records are not hosted on this public information website. The Squeebs financial application is not made publicly accessible by publishing this notice.

Scope of this website

These pages provide information about the owner's private use of Squeebs. They do not offer public registration or a service to third parties.
