Squeebs

Squeebs is a private, non-commercial personal-finance application used exclusively by its owner to organise financial transactions, track account balances, classify spending, and support personal cashflow planning and reporting.

Account information

Squeebs can import statements supplied by its owner and may use authorised account-information services, including Enable Banking, to retrieve balances and transactions from bank accounts the owner has explicitly connected.

Bank authorisation takes place through the provider and the financial institution. Squeebs does not provide payment initiation or make transfers.

Personal and local-first

The application and its financial data are maintained in the owner's private, self-hosted environment. Squeebs is not offered as a public financial service, and this website is not connected to the owner's financial database.

Privacy Notice Terms of Use

This public website contains information only. It does not provide access to financial accounts or the Squeebs application.
