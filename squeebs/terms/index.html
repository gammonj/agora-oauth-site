Private personal use

Squeebs is an owner-operated, non-commercial personal-finance tool. It is not offered to the public, and there is no public user-registration or account-access service. This website provides descriptive information and policy pages only.

Purpose and limitations

Squeebs helps its owner organise transactions, view financial information, classify spending, and prepare personal cashflow estimates. Its outputs are informational planning aids, not financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

The application does not initiate bank payments or transfer funds. Access to a bank's account information depends on the owner's separate consent and the bank's and relevant provider's terms.

Availability and data accuracy

Banking data, third-party interfaces and financial projections may be incomplete, delayed or unavailable. The owner remains responsible for checking information against bank and provider records before relying on it for financial decisions.
