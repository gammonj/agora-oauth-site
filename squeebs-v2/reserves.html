How reserves behave

Protect a purpose

Creating a target changes no balances. Allocating cash reduces the unallocated share available to normal runway.

Spend for the purpose

When a linked bill settles, the allocation is consumed alongside the actual cash payment. It must not count twice.

Prepare the next cycle

Annual or other recurring reserves can start a new cycle, without silently allocating fresh cash unless a rule explicitly allows it.
